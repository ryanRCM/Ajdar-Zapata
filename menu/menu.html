Food Allergies & Intolerances
Before you order, please tell a member of our staff about any allergy or intolerance. Every dish on this menu lists its main ingredients, and full information on the 14 major allergens is available on request. Our food is prepared in a kitchen where allergens are present, so we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from allergens.
